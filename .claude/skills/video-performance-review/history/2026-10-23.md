## GTA 6: the win was YouTube search for a brand-new term

`FXW9Zd5vjYk` "What Is Dacity Coin? The Crypto in GTA 6, Explained" has 619
views, 3.5x the previous best crypto long-form (First tx, 176). For the
first time, traffic sources were pulled (`insightTrafficSourceType`, a one-off
query, not yet in the CLI). Of the first 206 views: **YT_SEARCH 178**,
related 13, subscriber 6. Search terms: "dacity coin" 57, "dacity" 29,
"dacitycoin" 8, "dacity crypto" 5, "gta 6 meme coin" 4. The 10-15 launch
trailer had created the term five days before the upload, so almost no other
video answered it yet. Retention is healthy too: opens 97%, half gone at
28.7s, average view 1:24 (35.6%).

**The trend did nothing for the Short or the other platforms.** The Short
took 109 on YouTube (91 from the Shorts feed, 10 from search), 259 on TikTok
(the low batch), 4 on Instagram and 16/36 on Facebook. **So what worked was
not the trend.** What worked was a long-form whose title is the exact new
search term, in the few days before other videos answered it.

  check. These are compliant tests, not compliance failures.
- **Both cliffs land inside sentence 2 itself** (estimated at 3.25 words/s;
  the renders and SRTs were pruned). Bear falls 30 points from 5.0s to 6.0s,
  around "Stocks draw the line...". Stress-o-meter falls through 4-7s, while
  the viewer is being asked for a 0-10 number. Viewers left before the
  withheld answer could do its work. Two guesses at a cause, neither tested:
  Bear's sentence 2 opens on a different subject (stocks, in a crypto
  Short); Stress-o-meter's task asks for a number every sufferer already
  knows, while Jaw's task surprises ("are your teeth touching?").
- **Count across both runs:** 8 Shorts keep the answer back or overturn the
  title cleanly, and 6 of them hold (11.6-28.8s). 6 Shorts give the verdict,
  and none holds past 11.0s. GTA's reversal sits at 10.2s, in between. **So a
  withheld sentence 2 seems to be needed for a Short to hold, but it does not
  guarantee one.** The 10-11 "no overlap" line no longer stands.

  Market cap (8.0s) and Bear market (7.0s), both "What is [finance term]?",
