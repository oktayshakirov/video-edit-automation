- **Opener, for the same ledger:** the long form and the Short both open on

  Ulrich on screen as cards, plus Eric Clapton, Sting, Phil Collins, Neil
  Young and Ozzy Osbourne spoken in the long form. Every one is from the
  article's own table, each described only as having spoken publicly about
  having tinnitus. If any platform copy repeats a name, keep it to that.

  annual hearing test are the article's own and the disclaimer is already in
  the description's credits block.
