# Hal Finney — image credits

**There is exactly one free photograph of Hal Finney, and it is of a
teenager.** Wikimedia Commons has no category for him and no portrait from his
working life: a search returns one file of him, plus a set of unrelated
Historic American Buildings Survey photographs by a different Finney and a
screenshot of the first Bitcoin tweet (640x278, under the ~750px floor).

That is the whole reason `first-bitcoin-transaction` is cut the way it is. The
`satoshi-nakamoto` precedent applies — a subject with no usable picture gets
nobody standing in for him — so the long form uses this one file once, in a
beat's picture column under the line that dates it, and the rest of both cuts
is drawn beats and screened stock.

## Two constraints this file puts on any shot that uses it

- **It is square, so it may never fill a 16:9 frame.** `footage.md`'s rule on
  portrait and square sources applies: cover-cropping it to landscape throws
  away either his face or the sheet, which are the only two things in the
  picture. Picture column, or a fitted thumbnail crop.
- **L146 is a glare, not a grade.** It survives at 660px in a beat's picture
  column, where it is a downscale and framed. It would be the brightest thing
  in a gold-on-near-black video at full frame.

On the thumbnail the brightness is an asset rather than a problem — a grainy
1972 halftone is the most distinctive thing in a feed of glossy crypto
thumbnails — and the crop is `ax=0.12`, which keeps his whole head without
clipping it on the left edge and still carries a corner of the mathematics.
