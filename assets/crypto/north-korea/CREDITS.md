# north-korea

`dprk-flag.png` - the DPRK flag, drawn in code from its published
proportions (1:2; blue, white, red bands of 6/1/14/1/6; a red star in a white
disc). No source image, nothing to credit. Used as the ground of both covers
of the `north-korea-crypto` pair, because at 320px the competing covers all
say "North Korea" with the flag or a face before a word is read, and the
first cover (the card on dark water) said nothing until it was read.
