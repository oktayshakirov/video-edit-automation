# Ruja Ignatova — portrait credits

The site's own OG portrait (`crypto-wiki/public/images/crypto-ogs/ruja-ignatova.jpg`)
is 441x441 — far under the ~750px floor the blurred-fill layout and the
landscape Ken Burns shots need. A piece about a named person needs a real
picture of her, so these come from Wikimedia Commons.

`ruja-glamour.jpg` and `ruja-portrait.jpg` are the same OneCoin publicity
photograph at two crops. There is only this one press photo of her plus the FBI
poster, so a video about her has three distinct "her" frames total. The build
rule is: portrait and fbi once each, glamour twice — as a deliberate open/close
bookend, the same move the shared thumbnail makes. Never in adjacent shots.
