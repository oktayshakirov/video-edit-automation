# Vitalik Buterin — portrait credits

The site's own OG portrait (`crypto-wiki/public/images/crypto-ogs/vitalik-buterin.png`)
is 450x452 — under the ~750px floor the blurred-fill layout and the landscape
Ken Burns shots need. `crypto-wiki/public/images/posts/vitalik-buterin.jpg` is
960x540, usable only in a beat's 660px picture column. A piece about a named
person needs a real picture of him, so these come from Wikimedia Commons.

All three are crops of **one** press shoot — John Phillips photographing Buterin
at TechCrunch Disrupt London 2015, published on TechCrunch's Flickr. There is
no second well-licensed studio-quality photograph of him, so the pair has three
distinct "him" frames total (a genuine wide, a near-square, and a tight face),
the same constraint the Ruja and Saylor builds worked under.

The backdrop is a pale TechCrunch step-and-repeat — brighter and greener than
the channel's palette wants, but this is the subject's own portrait, the same
deliberate exception the Saylor and Ruja portraits take. Screened L141-151.

Build rule: `wide` for the landscape Ken Burns shots and the 16:9 thumbnail,
`square` for the Short's blurred-fill and the beat picture columns, `portrait`
for the Short's opening/closing face. Two crops of the one photo read as a
punch-in when they are three minutes (or half a Short) apart — never adjacent.

## Attribution block for the description

CC BY 2.0 requires attribution but is **not** share-alike, so the video is not
forced under a copyleft licence. This block goes in the description of anything
published (`Meta.credits` carries it):

> Photographs of Vitalik Buterin by John Phillips / TechCrunch, CC BY 2.0, via
> Wikimedia Commons (TechCrunch Disrupt London 2015).
> Ethereum mark by the Ethereum Foundation, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons
> (recoloured). Ethereum Classic mark: CC0.

CC BY is attribution-only and **not** share-alike, so neither video is pushed
under a copyleft licence — unlike the Ruja pair, which carries two CC BY-SA
photographs.
