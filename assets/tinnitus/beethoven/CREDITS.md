## Claims checked

Every quotation is Lady Wallace's 1866 translation, *Beethoven's Letters
1790-1826*, vol. 1 (Project Gutenberg 13065), read in the text, letters to
Wegeler of 29 June and 16 November 1801. Heller and Bergman 1953 (80 adults,
94%) and Del Bo et al. 2008 (53 young adults, 83% / 92%) are from the
article's PubMed sources. The cold baths are not used: the letter dismisses
the doctor who suggested them and never says Beethoven took them.
