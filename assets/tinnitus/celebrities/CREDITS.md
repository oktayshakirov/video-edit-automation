## What was checked, and against what

Each story was checked against public sources before it went in a script,
because the article hedges several of its own ("reportedly", "if accurate"):

- **Shatner and Nimoy** - a special-effects explosion while filming *Star Trek*
  ("Arena", 1967); Shatner's tinnitus is in his left ear, Nimoy's was in his
  right (Wikipedia, "Arena"). Shatner's own account is "we both got this ringing
  in our ears, and it never really went away" (Baltimore Sun, 1997). The ATA
  says he "considers himself habituated"; he calls it "many ups and downs".
  Where the blast was (soundstage or Vasquez Rocks) is disputed, so the scripts
  say "on set" and nothing more specific.
- **Steve Martin** - a gunfight scene on *Three Amigos* (1986); "You just get
  used to it" is widely attributed to him (Mental Floss, AOL) with no primary
  interview found. Quoted as his, nothing more.
- **Neil Young** - the 1991 *Ragged Glory* tour and mixing *Weld* left him with
  sound sensitivity and then tinnitus; *Harvest Moon* was quieter because he
  did not want to hear loud sounds; he still has mild tinnitus.
- **Barbra Streisand** - since childhood, starting in a classroom (Barbara
  Walters, 1985: age nine; her memoir: sixth grade). **The article's "around
  age seven" is not supported**, so the scripts say "as a child".
- **Dropped:** David Letterman (nothing found), Gerard Butler, Anthony Kiedis
  and Cher (hedged by the article itself). Shatner's description of suicidal
  thoughts is left out deliberately - it is true and it is not what a
  distressed viewer at two in the morning needs from a celebrity roundup.

## The closing list (added on review, 2026-10-09)

Names only, each with a public account of their own tinnitus: Pete Townshend,
Chris Martin, Lars Ulrich, Eric Clapton, Phil Collins and Ozzy Osbourne (all
checked for `tinnitus-in-musicians`), Jeff Beck (dated it to about 1990, a
drummer's cymbals), Liam Gallagher (The Sun, June 2013: "Without a doubt I have
tinnitus") and will.i.am (The Sun, 2010, and later interviews). **Left off:**
Sting (documented hearing loss, tinnitus only second-hand), James Hetfield and
Moby (fan and retailer pages only), Huey Lewis (Meniere's - a hearing story,
not a clean tinnitus one).
