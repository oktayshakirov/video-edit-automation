# OktAI - prompts for the logo and banner (ChatGPT image generation)

The look is the portfolio's: a near-black night sky, soft stars, one gold
accent. Generate, pick, then send the PNGs back - the engine takes the
wordmark and avatar from `assets/brand/oktai/`.

**Tips for ChatGPT images**

- Ask for one thing per image; regenerate rather than "fix" a busy result.
- Text in generated images often comes out wrong. If "OktAI" is misspelled,
  generate the icon **without** text and we set the wordmark in Poppins
  ourselves (that version already exists in the engine).
- Ask for a **transparent background** for the logo and avatar mark.
- No long dashes in any text on the images.

---

## 1. Avatar / profile picture (the octopus mark)

> A minimalist vector logo of a friendly, clever octopus, drawn with clean
> geometric lines in a modern flat style. The octopus is soft gold
> (gradient from #FFDB70 to #FFC05C) with subtle darker gold outlines. Its
> eight arms curl outward in a balanced, symmetrical circle, and each arm
> tip holds or forms a tiny tech symbol: a play button, a music note, a
> sound wave, a book, a code bracket, a bar chart, a phone, a drone
> propeller. Big calm eyes, a confident half smile, no mouth details.
> Centered composition, fits inside a circle, readable at 48 pixels.
> Background: deep night-sky navy black (#050507) with a few tiny soft
> white stars. No text. Flat vector illustration, crisp edges, no 3D, no
> photorealism, no gradients other than the gold.

Variant for a transparent PNG: replace the background line with
*"Transparent background, no stars."*

## 2. Wordmark logo

> A clean modern wordmark logo that reads exactly "oktAI". Lowercase "okt"
> in white, uppercase "AI" in a warm gold gradient (#FFDB70 to #FFC05C).
> Geometric sans serif typeface similar to Poppins SemiBold, tight but
> even letter spacing. A small minimalist gold octopus icon sits to the
> left of the word, the same height as the letters. Transparent
> background. Flat vector, no shadows, no 3D, no extra text.

## 3. YouTube banner (2560 x 1440, safe area 1546 x 423 in the centre)

> A wide YouTube channel banner, 2560 by 1440 pixels. Background: a calm
> night sky, near-black gradient from #050507 at the top to #0a0a0f at the
> bottom, scattered with small soft white stars of different sizes and a
> faint gold glow near the centre. In the exact centre, inside a band
> about 1546 by 423 pixels, the wordmark "oktAI" with "okt" in white and
> "AI" in a gold gradient (#FFDB70 to #FFC05C), geometric sans serif like
> Poppins SemiBold. Below it, smaller, in light grey (#D6D6D6): "OK, AI.
> Let's build." To the left of the wordmark, a minimalist flat gold
> octopus whose eight arms reach out into thin glowing gold lines that
> connect to small floating icons: a play button, a music note, a sound
> wave, a book, a code bracket, a bar chart, a phone, a drone. Lots of
> empty dark space at the edges. Flat vector style, elegant, minimal, no
> photorealism, no people.

If the text comes out wrong: ask for the same banner **without any text**,
and we place the wordmark and tagline on it ourselves.

## 3b. YouTube banner, "the one-person studio" (2026-11-25)

The avatar is now his own 3D cartoon (the channel picture), so the banner
stars him instead of the octopus. Attach the channel avatar image to the
chat as the reference, then:

> Use the attached avatar as the main character: keep his face, man bun,
> beard, black t-shirt and the same soft 3D cartoon style exactly. A wide
> panoramic YouTube channel banner, landscape. Scene: a calm night sky in
> near-black navy (#050507 to #0a0a0f) full of tiny soft stars. In the
> exact centre, the character sits relaxed at a small floating desk with a
> laptop, one hand on the keyboard, a mug of coffee beside it, looking
> pleased and a little mischievous. Behind and around him, a wide arc of
> floating rounded glass screens fans out like a constellation: a video
> editing timeline, an audio waveform, a code terminal with green text, a
> grid of video thumbnails, a rising bar chart, and a vertical phone
> showing a short video. The screens are joined by thin glowing gold lines
> (#FFC05C to #FFDB70) like a star map, and the lines meet at his laptop,
> as if he is conducting all of it. A tiny friendly gold robot arm reaches
> in from one screen to refill his coffee. To the left of him, large and
> clean, the word "oktAI" in a geometric sans serif like Poppins SemiBold:
> "okt" in white, "AI" in the gold gradient. Under it, smaller, in light
> grey: "OK, AI. Let's build." Keep the character, the word and the
> tagline inside a central horizontal band about 60% of the width and 30%
> of the height; the outer edges are only night sky and faint stars. Soft
> cinematic lighting from the screens, gold rim light on the character,
> crisp, premium, uncluttered. No real logos, no brand names, no other
> text, no other people.

Variant without text (when the letters come out wrong): replace the two
text sentences with *"No text anywhere in the image; leave calm empty sky
to the left of the character for a logo."* We set the wordmark ourselves.

Then send the PNG back: we extend it to 2560 x 1440 with more night sky and
check that everything sits inside YouTube's 1546 x 423 safe area (the strip
every device shows).
