## Formats, and whether they fit this channel

The reason it works comes first; the fit is judged against that reason and
against the no-financial-advice rule (`projects/crypto.md`).

| format | why its audience clicks | fit here | seen failing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "[Named project] - scam or the real thing?" | they are deciding whether to trust it | **yes**, as long as the verdict judges a *claim* with evidence, never the asset as an investment | not yet looked |
| "What is [brand-new term]? [Familiar name], explained" | nobody else answers it yet | **yes** - E8, our only search win | not yet looked |
| "If you don't understand [mechanism], you don't understand [Bitcoin / crypto]" | the mechanism is unfamiliar, the subject is what they care about | **yes** - promises understanding. Candidates: custody, finality, slippage, the mempool | not yet looked |
| "[Old thing] is [CAPS]. [New thing] isn't." | a big shift, stated with confidence | **careful** - a claim about the world, fine; about a price, never | not yet looked |
| "The new era of [X] has just begun" / "[X] changed and nobody noticed" | urgency, being early | **careful** - only for a structural change that has already happened (a law, an ETF), never a prediction | not yet looked |
| "Brutally honest [X] in 7 minutes" | candour, and a bounded time cost | **yes** - fits the sceptical register | not yet looked |
| "[Antagonist] is coming for YOUR [thing]" | a threat to their own stake | **careful** - true and sourced only; fear without a mechanism is the pitch register we avoid | not yet looked |
| "The real reason [X] exploded today" | today's move | **no** - news and price | - |
| "so fast it feels like cheating" | a wanted result, faster than expected | **no** - implies gains | - |
| "How to launch a meme coin" / "how I rug pull a meme coin (+$N)" | they want to run a launch or a rug pull themselves | **no** - the audience wants to commit the fraud our videos warn about; ranking there is impressions, never viewers (Meme coins: 11,478 impressions, 0.05% CTR) | - |
| "I did N of X and learned this" | proof by volume of experience | **no** - a faceless channel has no N to claim; never invent one | - |
