## Our version - keep, cannot have, replaced by, rule limits
- **Keep:** the ranking, the faces, the numbers drawn to scale (`bars`),
  the year in the title ("2026").
- **Cannot have:** a list with no argument - a faceless channel's narration
  over a list is exactly the failing shape.
- **Replaced by:** the mechanism nobody on the page explains - the biggest
  fortunes are stakes in the businesses everyone uses (an exchange's fee,
  a stablecoin's interest), and every figure is a stake times a guess
  (the new `stake` beat; Forbes against Bloomberg on CZ). Satoshi as the
  planted payoff.
- **Rule limits:** no "copy the billionaires" advice (the article's
  dollar-cost-averaging close is dropped), every figure dated and sourced,
  no fortune presented as fact.
