## The promise
A secret plan with a huge number on it: the government is about to push
its debt onto you through stablecoins. The viewer has heard the rumour (a
Putin adviser said it in September 2025) and wants to know if it is real.
Problem-aware, and suspicious of both sides.

## The opening (promise / proof / plan, with times)
Clearly Finance: the debt figure and "foreign buyers are walking away" in
the first ten seconds, "and yes, it involves cryptocurrency" at 0:25, the
Putin adviser named at 1:01 as the source of the claim, then a promise to
show a historical "playbook" first. No proof of the narrator. Lorenzana:
"a theory going around online" at 0:00, the rumour stated in his own words
by 0:30, his credential (CPA) in the channel name.

## The structure (chapters, points, length, wpm, the turn, the CTA)
Clearly Finance: 13:10, 1,859 words, 141 wpm, seven chapters - debt,
1933 / 1971 / QE devaluations, the "Trojan horse" (issuers must buy
Treasuries; Tether and Circle among the largest holders), a three-phase
"reset", signals to watch, then hard-asset advice. The turn is at 6:12:
the GENIUS Act makes stablecoins automatic buyers of US debt. Lorenzana:
13:47, 2,046 words, eleven chapters, three rumours "debunked" one by one,
then "what's the real plan".

## What satisfied viewers (comments)
Clearly Finance's top comments argue the arithmetic, not the plot: "you
cannot inflate away an exponentially growing debt", and one does the sum -
"$4T in stablecoins at a 20% devaluation only helps $800B; the debt will be
$45T". Lorenzana's top comment (175 likes) is a fear about who controls
the coins, not the debt: company coins merging until you are "trapped in a
system you HAVE to use". Both comment sections are about **control and the
numbers**, which the rumour itself never gives.

## Our version - keep, cannot have, replaced by, rule limits
- **Keep:** the rumour's own question as the long-form title (it is the
  search), the adviser's real words on screen early, the mechanism at the
  centre (the 6:12 peak), and a list of claims checked one at a time
  (Lorenzana's shape).
- **Cannot have:** a presenter, a credential, a "how to protect yourself"
  section - that is direction (hard assets, defensive stocks) and closed
  to us.
- **Replaced by:** the sum the comments asked for, drawn - all stablecoins
  ($287B) against the debt ($40.6T), under one percent; the interest drawn
  going to the issuer, which is the true part neither video says plainly;
  the law's own text captured; the freeze, which is the "control" fear
  answered with a sourced number.
- **Rule limits:** no direction, no "what to buy". Verdicts judge claims
  ("it erases the debt" - no; "it funds the debt" - yes), never an asset.
  The adviser's claim is quoted as his, with date and place.
