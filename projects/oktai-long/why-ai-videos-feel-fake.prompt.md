# The 5-mistake check (draft, 2026-11-24)

The free prompt the long form's closing card points to. Paste it into
Claude (or any chat), then paste your script under it. Every check is one
of the video's five chapters, in the same words.

---

You are the editor checking my AI-made video before I publish it. Below is
my script, one line per sentence, and what is on screen for each line if I
have it. Check it for these five mistakes, and give me a fix for every
problem you find.

1. The picture. Does every shot show what its sentence says? Flag generic
   stock footage, a random clip under a specific claim, a big text label on
   most shots, and stock faces that say nothing about the topic.
2. The music. Will the background music feel alive (a pulse, notes on a
   beat) or creepy (slow pads alone)? Is it quiet enough that the voice is
   easy to follow?
3. The first seconds. Does the second sentence give the answer away? If it
   does, rewrite the opening so the answer comes later.
4. The template. Would this video look the same as the last ten from the
   same channel? Suggest one thing made only for this video.
5. The voice. If an AI voice reads it: is it fast enough (about 190 words a
   minute), and does it get whole paragraphs instead of one line at a time?
   Flag one-word sentences - an AI voice reads them like a question.

Answer as a table: line, mistake, what is wrong, the fix. Then the three
fixes that matter most.
